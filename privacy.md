# Privacy Policy for My Short Agent

Effective date: 1 November 2026

My Short Agent ("the app") is a personal tool its owner uses to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with that information.

## YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

## What the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel and give permission, the app can:

- upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and privacy status
- read the channel's basic details and its list of videos
- read YouTube Analytics reports for the channel, such as views, watch time, retention and subscribers gained

The app also reads public YouTube data, such as search results and public video statistics, to study trends.

The app never asks for or stores your Google password.

## How the information is used

The information is used only to run the connected channels: to upload approved Shorts, measure how they perform, and choose what to make next. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## Where it is stored and for how long

- Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted in Google Cloud Secret Manager, in the owner's Google Cloud project.
- Analytics and video statistics are stored in the owner's Google Cloud project (India region) while the channel stays connected. The app checks at least every 30 days that it is still allowed to access them.
- Other YouTube data, including public data, is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
- Backups are kept in the owner's Google Cloud project and are deleted along with the main data.

## Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or give your data to anyone for their own use. It sends data only to services that help it run:

- Google Cloud, which hosts the app
- the Google Gemini API, which receives video ideas, scripts and summary statistics to help write and plan Shorts
- Telegram, which delivers status reports to the owner

No third party serves ads through the app. Neither the app nor this website places cookies on your device.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

My Short Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access and deleting data

You can remove the app's access at any time on the [Google security settings page](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). You can also email the address below to ask for your data to be deleted. After access is removed, all data the app got from that channel is deleted within 7 days. If a sign-in token stops working, the related data is deleted within 30 days.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
